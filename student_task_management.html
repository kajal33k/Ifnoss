Student Portal

Student Portal Software

Student portal is a login for students where they can track all the information related to them in the records of the university / college. Here students are notified with all the course related information to them. This easy to use tool handles attendance data, invoices, semester exams and results, event calendar and update with latest notification from university/college authority. Given below are some of the basic features of student login module system and many more advanced one are in stock pipeline.

Student portal outlooks the following information

Viewing

    • Personal details along with photograph
    • Attendance
    • Fees due & fees deposited
    • Time table
    • Syllabus
    • Attendance
    • Assignments
    • Exam schedule
    • Internal & external marks
    • Holiday calendar
    • Academic schedule
    • News & notices

Downloading -

    • Syllabus
    • Course material, Term papers, Assignments & Question bank
    • Certificates & admit cards
    • Mark sheet
    Library
      • View library books issued
      • Search books and reserve
      • List of New arrivals

      Online fee payment

      VISA / Immigration Compliance
